# GrowthCrew

## Register

product

## Users

Teams creating social posts and finding businesses for email outreach.

## Product Purpose

A shared workspace for AI captions, media, social scheduling and publishing, business discovery, personalized email drafts, and reply tracking. The landing page introduces both workflows before sign-in.

## Brand Personality

Inventive, direct, expressive. GrowthCrew is a product by In2Peta. The supplied In2Peta lightbulb logo is the brand asset.

## Anti-references

Generic repeated feature cards, decorative metrics, invented social proof, excessive glow, and unreadable dark-theme labels. Use the LeadsAgent reference for the main landing skeleton, adapted to GrowthCrew's broader offering.

## Design Principles

- Make the two product entry paths clear.
- Use the supplied identity across marketing and workspace screens.
- Show actual capabilities and preserve working behavior.
- Give content a clear reading order through scale and grouping.
- Keep repeated workspace actions fast and predictable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable text contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
